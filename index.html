Hi, I'm Zaira. I say all of this with love, and with the full support of my own research.

I live, I laugh, I love. Yes, it's a sign in a kitchen. No, I won't be taking questions.

I design logos, posters, websites and clothes, and something I'm generously calling Instagram. I do brand strategy, which is taste with footnotes. I write on a Substack, for the thoughts that won't fit inside a logo.

I'm also building a hospitality brand from nothing, financial plan included, to prove that a restaurant can feel like a feeling. (It can.)

Nothing in nature is a rectangle, so nothing in this portfolio is one, apart from a single floor you'll find under Work. That floor is a cry for help, and I stand by it.

It looks effortless. It was not. If you know, you know. If you don't, hire me and find out.
