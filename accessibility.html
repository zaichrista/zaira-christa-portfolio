Accessibility

Last updated: 3 October 2026

I want everyone to be able to use this site. It's deliberately playful, which can make accessibility harder, so here is where it stands honestly.

What works

  • Pages use real headings, buttons, links and form labels, and the page language is set.
  • The photo has a text description.
  • Main text is dark on a light background, or light on dark, with strong contrast.
  • The contact form works through your own email app, so you can use whatever tools you already rely on.
  • Some animations are switched off for visitors who've asked their device for reduced motion.

Known problems

  • Some of the playful interactions (squishing the letters, dragging sticky notes into the bin, dragging windows around) rely on a mouse or touch, and don't have a full keyboard equivalent.
  • The home-page headline is made of animated, flashing letters. It can be distracting, and the flashing may be uncomfortable for people sensitive to it.
  • Some small, grey label text has lower contrast than I'd like.
  • The About and Contact pages scale themselves to fit the window. On very small screens or at high zoom, text can become small.
  • Text in the work presentations is shown inside styled windows, and I haven't yet tested it with every screen reader.

I'm working through these, and I'd rather hear about the ones I've missed.

Tell me about a problem

If something on this site is hard to use, or you'd like any of the content in another format, email zcbarakat@gmail.com and I'll reply within a few working days and sort out a fix or an alternative.

Standard I'm aiming for

I'm aiming for the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 level AA. I haven't had the site independently audited, so I can't claim full compliance. The notes above are from my own checks.
